CASE SUMMARY FROM TRIAL MONITORING
Case: Republic of Calderra v. Esme Rook (Mirevo District Court, Criminal Chamber, case no. MDC-2023-0064)
Presiding Judge: Anwar Selik
Charge: Dissemination of false information (Penal Code Art. 214)

At the first appearance on 11 April 2023, the court refused the request for pretrial detention and released Esme Rook on bail. All hearings were held in public. The court excluded the evidence that the defence had contested. The court denied the defence motion to adjourn the trial.

On 29 August 2023, the court convicted Esme Rook and imposed a sentence of 24 months' imprisonment.
