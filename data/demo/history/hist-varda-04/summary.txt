CASE SUMMARY FROM TRIAL MONITORING
Case: Republic of Calderra v. Sefa Morane (Mirevo District Court, Criminal Chamber, case no. MDC-2024-0009)
Presiding Judge: Ilena Varda
Charge: Dissemination of false information (Penal Code Art. 214)

At the first appearance on 22 January 2024, the court ordered that Sefa Morane be detained pending trial. All hearings were held in public. The court admitted the evidence that the defence had contested.

On 14 May 2024, the court convicted Sefa Morane and imposed a sentence of 24 months' imprisonment.
