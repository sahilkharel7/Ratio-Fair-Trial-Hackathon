CASE SUMMARY FROM TRIAL MONITORING
Case: Republic of Calderra v. Jonas Petrel (Mirevo District Court, Criminal Chamber, case no. MDC-2023-0142)
Presiding Judge: Iléna Várda
Charge: Dissemination of false information (Penal Code Art. 214)

At the first appearance on 14 September 2023, the court ordered that Jonas Petrel be detained pending trial. The court closed part of the trial to the public at the request of the prosecution. The court denied the defence motion to adjourn the trial.

On 30 January 2024, the court convicted Jonas Petrel and imposed a sentence of 42 months' imprisonment.
