CASE SUMMARY FROM TRIAL MONITORING
Case: Republic of Calderra v. Farid Moss (Mirevo District Court, Criminal Chamber, case no. MDC-2023-0151)
Presiding Judge: Anwar Selik
Charge: Dissemination of false information (Penal Code Art. 214)

At the first appearance on 2 October 2023, the court ordered that Farid Moss be detained pending trial. All hearings were held in public. The court granted the defence request to call an additional witness.

On 13 February 2024, the court convicted Farid Moss and imposed a sentence of 36 months' imprisonment.
