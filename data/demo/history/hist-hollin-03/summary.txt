Case: Republic of Calderra v. Bo Lindqvar (Mirevo District Court, Criminal Chamber, case no. MDC-2024-0021)

Charge: Dissemination of false information (Penal Code Art. 214)

At the first appearance on 15 February 2024, the court ordered that Bo Lindqvar be detained pending trial. All hearings were held in public. The court denied the defence motion to adjourn the trial.
