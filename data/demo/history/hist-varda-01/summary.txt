CASE SUMMARY FROM TRIAL MONITORING
Case: Republic of Calderra v. Aren Holt (Mirevo District Court, Criminal Chamber, case no. MDC-2023-0031)
Presiding Judge: Ilena Varda
Charge: Dissemination of false information (Penal Code Art. 214)

At the first appearance on 10 February 2023, the court ordered that Aren Holt be detained pending trial. The court closed part of the trial to the public at the request of the prosecution. The court admitted the evidence that the defence had contested. The court denied the defence motion to adjourn the trial.

On 20 June 2023, the court convicted Aren Holt and imposed a sentence of 36 months' imprisonment.
