CASE SUMMARY FROM TRIAL MONITORING
Case: Republic of Calderra v. Ivo Saren (Mirevo District Court, Criminal Chamber, case no. MDC-2023-0050)
Presiding Judge: Petra Hollin
Charge: Dissemination of false information (Penal Code Art. 214)

At the first appearance on 20 March 2023, the court ordered that Ivo Saren be detained pending trial. All hearings were held in public. The court admitted the evidence that the defence had contested.

On 18 July 2023, the court convicted Ivo Saren and imposed a sentence of 24 months' imprisonment.
