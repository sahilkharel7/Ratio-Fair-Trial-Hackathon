CASE SUMMARY FROM TRIAL MONITORING
Case: Republic of Calderra v. Hugo Pell (Mirevo District Court, Criminal Chamber, case no. MDC-2024-0112)
Presiding Judge: Anwar Selik
Charge: Dissemination of false information (Penal Code Art. 214)

At the first appearance on 9 September 2024, the court refused the request for pretrial detention and released Hugo Pell on bail. All hearings were held in public. The court admitted the evidence that the defence had contested. The court denied the defence motion to adjourn the trial.

On 14 January 2025, the court convicted Hugo Pell and imposed a sentence of 30 months' imprisonment.
