Case no. MDC-2025-0147
The Republic of Calderra v. Daro Venn
Date: 19 March 2025
Judge: Ilena Varda

I. REQUEST

The Public Prosecutor requests that the detention of the accused be extended by two months.

II. APPLICABLE LAW

Article 167(1) of the Code of Criminal Procedure provides: "Detention pending trial may be ordered where there is a reasonable suspicion that the accused committed the offence and a risk that he will flee, interfere with the evidence or commit a further offence."

III. GROUNDS

The accused has relatives in a neighbouring country and travelled there twice in the past year, so there is a risk that he will flee. The forensic examination of the telephone seized from the accused has not yet been completed, and its results are needed to establish when the articles were prepared. Since the first order, two of the officials expected to testify have reported being approached by acquaintances of the accused, which shows that the risk of interference persists. The Court has considered whether house arrest or a duty to report to the police would be sufficient, and finds that neither would prevent such approaches.

IV. DECISION

The detention of the accused is extended until 12 May 2025.

(signed) Ilena Varda
