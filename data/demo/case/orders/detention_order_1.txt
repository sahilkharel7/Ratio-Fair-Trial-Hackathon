The Republic of Calderra v. Daro Venn

I. REQUEST

The Public Prosecutor requests that the accused be detained pending trial.

II. APPLICABLE LAW

Article 167(1) of the Code of Criminal Procedure provides: "Detention pending trial may be ordered where there is a reasonable suspicion that the accused committed the offence and a risk that he will flee, interfere with the evidence or commit a further offence."

III. GROUNDS

There is a reasonable suspicion that the accused published articles containing false information about the Ministry of Water that caused public alarm. The accused has relatives in a neighbouring country and travelled there twice in the past year, so there is a risk that he will flee. The accused knows the officials of the Ministry of Water who are expected to testify, and there is a risk that he will seek to influence their statements. The offence carries a penalty of up to six years of imprisonment.

IV. DECISION

The accused shall be detained pending trial until 19 March 2025.
