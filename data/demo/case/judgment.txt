MIREVO DISTRICT COURT, CRIMINAL CHAMBER

Case no. MDC-2025-0147
The Republic of Calderra v. Daro Venn
Delivered on 14 July 2025
Presiding Judge: Ilena Varda
Lay assessors: Oren Valk and Teja Morrow

I. PROCEDURAL HISTORY

The accused was arrested on 14 February 2025 and was brought before this Court on 19 February 2025, which ordered his detention pending trial. His detention was extended on 19 March 2025 and again on 14 May 2025. The indictment was filed on 3 March 2025. The trial opened on 2 June 2025, and the Court held four hearings.

II. THE CHARGE

The accused is charged with disseminating false information likely to cause public alarm, an offence under Article 214(2) of the Penal Code.

III. SUBMISSIONS OF THE PARTIES

The prosecution argues that the accused knew that the allegations were false and that his conduct was intended to undermine public confidence in the institutions of the State. The defence contends that the messages relied on by the prosecution were extracted from a telephone seized without a judicial warrant and must be excluded. The defence further argues that the first article was published before the amendment to Article 214 entered into force.

IV. THE APPLICABLE LAW

Article 214(2) of the Penal Code provides: "Whoever disseminates information that he knows to be false, where such dissemination is likely to cause public alarm, shall be punished by imprisonment of two to six years."

V. ASSESSMENT OF THE COURT

Between 9 February and 12 February 2025, the accused published a series of articles on the news portal Mirevo Today alleging that officials of the Ministry of Water had diverted public funds intended for the northern irrigation project. The articles were shared more than 40,000 times within three days and caused public alarm in the northern districts, where residents gathered outside the regional water office on 13 February 2025. The accused knew that the allegations were false, because the Ministry had published the audited project accounts on 2 February 2025, a week before the first article appeared.

Messages retrieved from the telephone of the accused demonstrate that he arranged when the articles would appear together with individuals outside the country, so as to increase their effect.

The Court has examined the testimony of the two officials of the Ministry of Water, each of whom confirmed that the published figures did not match the audited accounts. The Court considers their testimony credible and consistent.

As to the defence argument that the first article predates the amendment to Article 214, the Court notes that the amendment entered into force on 1 January 2025, while the first article appeared on 9 February 2025. That argument is therefore rejected.

The Court finds that the conduct of the accused was deliberate and was intended to undermine public confidence in the institutions of the State.

In determining the sentence, the Court has taken into account that the accused has no previous convictions.

VI. DISPOSITION

For these reasons, the Court finds the accused Daro Venn guilty of the offence under Article 214(2) of the Penal Code and sentences him to four years' imprisonment. The time spent in pretrial detention since 19 February 2025 shall be deducted from the sentence.

(signed) Ilena Varda, Presiding Judge
