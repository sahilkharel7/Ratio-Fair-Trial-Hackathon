OFFICE OF THE PUBLIC PROSECUTOR OF MIREVO
INDICTMENT
Case no. MDC-2025-0147
The Republic of Calderra v. Daro Venn
Mirevo, 3 March 2025

I. THE ACCUSED

Daro Venn, born on 11 May 1987 in Halvar, journalist, residing in Mirevo.

II. PROCEDURAL HISTORY

The accused was arrested at his home in Mirevo in the early morning of 14 February 2025. On 15 February 2025 the accused was brought before the Public Prosecutor, who ordered that he remain in police custody. On 19 February 2025 the accused was brought before Judge Ilena Varda of the Mirevo District Court, who ordered his detention pending trial. On 3 March 2025 the Public Prosecutor filed this indictment with the Mirevo District Court.

III. STATEMENT OF FACTS

Between 9 February and 12 February 2025, the accused published a series of articles on the news portal Mirevo Today alleging that officials of the Ministry of Water had diverted public funds intended for the northern irrigation project. The articles were shared more than 40,000 times within three days and caused public alarm in the northern districts, where residents gathered outside the regional water office on 13 February 2025. The accused knew that the allegations were false, because the Ministry had published the audited project accounts on 2 February 2025, a week before the first article appeared. Messages recovered from the accused's mobile telephone show that he coordinated the timing of the publications with persons abroad in order to maximise their impact. The conduct of the accused was deliberate and was intended to undermine public confidence in the institutions of the State.

IV. EVIDENCE

The prosecution relies on the articles published on Mirevo Today, the audited accounts of the northern irrigation project, the testimony of two officials of the Ministry of Water and of two police investigators, and the messages recovered from the accused's mobile telephone.

V. LEGAL QUALIFICATION

Article 214(2) of the Penal Code provides: "Whoever disseminates information that he knows to be false, where such dissemination is likely to cause public alarm, shall be punished by imprisonment of two to six years."

VI. CHARGE

The accused is charged with disseminating false information likely to cause public alarm, an offence under Article 214(2) of the Penal Code.

The Public Prosecutor requests that the accused be found guilty and sentenced to imprisonment.

(signed) Malik Ferrow, Public Prosecutor of Mirevo
