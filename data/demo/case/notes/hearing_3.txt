TRIAL MONITORING NOTE
Case: Republic of Calderra v. Daro Venn (Mirevo District Court, Criminal Chamber, case no. MDC-2025-0147)
Hearing: 3
Hearing date: 30 June 2025
Monitor: R.T.

The hearing began at 10:00 before Presiding Judge Ilena Varda.

Mr. Venn was present in the dock when the hearing opened. Defence counsel Lena Brask was present.

The presiding judge ruled that the messages taken from Mr. Venn's telephone were admissible, without giving reasons. Ms. Brask's objection was recorded.

At the prosecution's request, the presiding judge ordered the public and journalists to leave the courtroom during the testimony of a police investigator, citing national security. The monitor was also asked to leave and could not observe that part of the hearing.

When the public was readmitted, a second police investigator testified about the analysis of the telephone. Ms. Brask questioned him about how the messages had been extracted.

Defence counsel asked the court to hear two witnesses, both editors at Mirevo Today, who had attended the editorial meeting at which the articles were approved. The presiding judge refused to hear them, without giving reasons.

Defence counsel argued that the first article was published before the amendment to Article 214 entered into force, so that Mr. Venn could not be punished for conduct that was lawful at the time.

The hearing ended at 14:20.
