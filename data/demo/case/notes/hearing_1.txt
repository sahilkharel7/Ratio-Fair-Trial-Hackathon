The trial opened on 2 June 2025 at 10:05 before Presiding Judge Ilena Varda, sitting with two lay assessors. About twenty members of the public and three journalists attended, and the courtroom doors remained open.

Mr. Venn was present in the courtroom throughout the hearing, seated in a glass dock. Defence counsel Lena Brask, whom Mr. Venn had retained himself, was present and sat beside the dock.

According to the case file and to his counsel, Mr. Venn's first language is Ostric; he grew up in the Ostric-speaking north of the country.

The presiding judge read out the charge under Article 214(2) of the Penal Code. Mr. Venn confirmed that he had received the indictment on 4 March 2025.

Ms. Brask told the court that she had received the 900-page case file only three days before this hearing and asked for an adjournment of two weeks to prepare the defence. The presiding judge refused the request and ordered the trial to continue.

Ms. Brask reminded the court that Mr. Venn had been arrested at his home before dawn on 14 February 2025 and was questioned by police for two days. She stated that she was first able to meet Mr. Venn on 21 February 2025, a week after his arrest.

The presiding judge informed Mr. Venn that he had the right to remain silent. Mr. Venn chose to make a statement, in which he denied the charge and said that his articles were based on documents given to him by employees of the Ministry of Water.

The hearing ended at 13:40.
