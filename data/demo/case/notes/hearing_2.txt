The hearing began at 10:10 before Presiding Judge Ilena Varda. The courtroom was open to the public.

Mr. Venn was present in the dock for the whole hearing. Defence counsel Lena Brask was present and represented him.

Two officials of the Ministry of Water testified for the prosecution. Both said that the figures in Mr. Venn's articles did not match the audited project accounts. Ms. Brask cross-examined both witnesses about who had prepared the audit.

Defence counsel argued that the messages presented by the prosecution had been extracted from Mr. Venn's mobile telephone, which police seized at his home on 14 February 2025 without a judicial warrant, and asked the court to exclude them as evidence.
